HOW TO READ THIS PAGE

  • DAILY: opens on the latest date; trend = latest date − 7 days. Pick another date (calendar or ◀ ▶): trend = that date ± 7 days.
  • MONTHLY: opens on the latest month (1st → latest date); trend = every day of the month. Pick a month and a date: period = 1st of that month → that date (e.g. July + 17-Aug = 01-Jul → 17-Aug); trend = every day of the picked month.
  • YEARLY: 01-Apr → latest date (or picked date); trend = one bar per month up to that month.
  • FGD availability = FGD run hours ÷ unit run hours × 100. Unit run hours not entered = 24 h; entered hours are used automatically.
  • SOx removal efficiency = (inlet − outlet) ÷ inlet × 100, worked out daily. FGD run hours = 0 → FGD NOT IN SERVICE; such days are left out of SOx averages.
  • APC % = FGD MU consumption ÷ unit MU production × 100 (4 decimals); energy in MU and MWh.
  • Data comes live from the FGD logbook Google Sheet (DAILY_ENTRY, TRUCKS, DEFECTS) and refreshes about every 5 minutes. If the Google Sheet cannot be reached, the last Excel uploaded with the password (button at the bottom) is shown.
